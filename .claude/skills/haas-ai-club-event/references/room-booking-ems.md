# Room requests on Haas EMS

Club event rooms at Haas are requested through the Haas EMS room system behind
CalNet + Duo. Submissions are **requests** - EMS confirms later by email. Never
treat a submission as a booking.

## Process

0. **Already booked?** If the organizer says a room is confirmed, do NOT submit
   a new request: verify the existing reservation (confirmation email or EMS
   record) actually covers the room, date and time needed, including setup
   buffer. Only request when there is no booking or the booking does not match.
1. Sign in to Haas EMS with the organizer's CalNet credentials (from the harness
   secret store - see credentials-and-access.md). Expect a Duo push; warn the
   organizer it's coming and that pushes expire fast. Sessions also expire
   quickly - don't stall mid-flow.
2. Create a reservation request with the required fields: attendee count,
   the club it is for (Haas AI Club), and whether anyone under 18 attends.
   Book under the series name when the event belongs to one (e.g. "lunch and
   learn series").
3. Hold the room from setup time if the organizer asked for setup buffer - but
   keep the published event time unchanged on the poster, RSVP and marketing.
4. Record the request ID and wait for the confirmation email. Only then put the
   room on public copy.
5. For moves or special asks (e.g. after a reservation is locked), email
   roomres@haas.berkeley.edu from the organizer's account - with their approval
   of the draft first.

| Room | Style | Capacity |
|---|---|---|
| N100 | tiered | 141 |
| N500 | large flat | 78 |
| N300 | tiered | 76 |
| N270 | tiered | 57 |
| C250 | flat | 26 |

Only N300 and N100 fit a ~70-person tiered ask. Group study rooms (N355 fits 6,
N358 fits 9) are a different system and not for events.

## Constraints to surface, not assume

- Rooms are first-come-first-served; academic courses take priority, so a
  recurring club hold is not guaranteed.
- EMS may not do standing/recurring bookings - roomres@haas.berkeley.edu
  answers that.
- Classrooms have historically entered a restricted period around December to
  mid-February (observed 2026). Check current Haas space-management guidance
  before promising a winter date: haas.berkeley.edu/events-space-management/how-to-book-spaces/
