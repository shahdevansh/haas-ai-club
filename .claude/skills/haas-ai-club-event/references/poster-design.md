# Poster design language

Club preference (organizer's stated design rules). Official assets live in the
club's Figma board ("Logo & Assets",
https://www.figma.com/design/dUNoWBVbuX4Xqbkrzrx15R/Logo---Assets) - the latest
assets there are the official ones. Use them rather than recreating logos.

## Content: keep it minimal

- Location, timing, event headline, the official Haas AI Club logo.
- At most one extra line on what the event is about. Do not add more text.
- Room number on the poster, with no "tentative" wording - if the room is not
  confirmed, leave it off and add it after EMS confirms.
- Speaker names carry class year for students (e.g. "FTMBA 27").
- Panels may show an industry layer per company (Memory / Model / Agents / Chips).
- Headshots: all speakers or none - keep it consistent across the poster.

## Layout

- Logo small, at the top, between two circles.
- Header and sub-headers moved up; date + venue given breathing room.
- Bottom half in two columns: QR code on the right, logos/photos of who is
  involved on the left (speaker photo + company logos for a talk; partner club
  logo for a mixer; "Speakers from" + company logos for a summit).
- QR codes square and aligned across the set, generated only from the VERIFIED
  public RSVP link (see campusgroups-event.md for the draft-first sequence).
- Fonts: Roboto default; Quicksand as the alternate option.
- Spacing (standing rule): more padding between the description and the yellow
  divider line; less padding between the title and the date/venue lines.
- Promo lines (e.g. venue perk + "Limited spots") merge into one block, same
  font, slightly smaller, no extra padding between them. Short punchy hooks
  over descriptive taglines. Make it tasteful.

## Process

- The poster is NOT a blocker for the RSVP: the CampusGroups draft comes first
  (to yield the public link), the QR comes from that link, then the poster is
  finalized and attached to the draft before publishing.
- Produce two simple options and let the organizer pick. Expect rapid one-line
  edits; re-render after each.
- When the venue, time or title changes, update everything derived from it in
  the same pass: poster, CampusGroups flyer/banner/title/location/description,
  website entry, and any queued marketing copy.

## Series pattern: Berkeley AI Risk Speaker Series (approved 2026-11-09)

For Haas AI Club x AI Risk Speaker Series co-hosted talks (first use: Emma
Pierson, Nov 17 2026), the organizer approved this poster pattern ("Same kinda
poster for future AI Risk Speaker series events"):

- Kicker: "AI RISK SPEAKER SERIES"; headline = the talk title.
- Where the usual one-line "what it's about" sits (under date/venue), use
  TOPIC SUBTEXT drawn from the talk abstract on ai-risk.berkeley.edu - never
  invented. Co-host credit does NOT go in that line.
- Haas AI Club logo at the top only; do NOT repeat it in the bottom strip.
- Bottom strip, full width, ONE row: "CO-SPONSORED BY" + the four co-sponsor
  logos from the bottom of ai-risk.berkeley.edu (CDSS, Kavli Center, Berkeley
  Risk & Security Lab, BASIS). Download the actual assets:
  assets/logos/cdss.svg, assets/logos/brsl.svg, assets/logos/basis-bear-lockup.png;
  Kavli Center has no image asset - render its wordmark as text
  ("Kavli Center" / "for Ethics, Science, and the Public").
- Site link "ai-risk.berkeley.edu" as a text line at the very bottom.
- Speaker block (photo, name, role) stays bottom-left; QR bottom-right.
- Evan Bright (BASIS, EWMBA '28) coordinates the series on the co-host side and
  supplies official art assets; swap recreated marks for his official assets
  when they arrive.

CampusGroups for this series: RSVP cap 50 max (organizer's rule, overrides the
2x-room heuristic).

Fonts for this series: QUICKSAND is the approved default (organizer chose the
non-Roboto option for the Emma Pierson poster, 2026-11-09); offer Roboto as the
alternate.
