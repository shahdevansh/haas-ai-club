# Optional member email blast

Offer this in the plan; skip it silently if the organizer does not want it.

## Shape

Short and crisp, with the poster attached:

- Subject: <event title> + date.
- Body: the marketing message content (title, time, venue, 2-3 why-it-matters
  lines, RSVP link) in email form. Greeting, the essentials, the link, sign-off.
- Attachment: the final poster.
- From the club/organizer account, to the member list.

## Rules

- Draft first, always. The organizer approves the exact words, recipient list
  and attachment before anything sends.
- Use the member list from the club's own roster source; BCC a list rather than
  exposing addresses, unless the platform sends individual copies.
- Attach the actual final poster file - open it first to confirm it is the
  right version.
