# Reimbursements

How club expenses paid personally get paid back. **Everything in this file is
"as observed in Fall 2026" - verify the current rules against the official
sources below before every filing. Forms, fields, and payout rules change; if
you cannot verify an item, say so explicitly instead of relying on this file.**

## Where to verify (discovery starting points - NOT verified current links)

These are starting points for finding the current rules, not endorsements of
any specific page, form, or policy as current. Find the live page, read it,
and record what you verified and on what date. If you cannot verify an item,
say so rather than relying on this file.

- CalLink (the UC Berkeley student-org platform) - the purchase-request form
  itself is the source of truth for required fields and attachments.
- ASUC / LEAD Center funding and reimbursement guidance - payout method rules,
  EFT authorization, check pickup hours and location.
- Haas finance/operations guidance for club purchases - vendor and food rules.
- The club's own finance officer - for anything the sources leave ambiguous.

## The flow (as observed Fall 2026 - re-verify)

1. **File a CalLink purchase request (reimbursement) - the formal portal, not
   an email to an officer.** CalLink sits behind CalNet + Duo; the payee's
   student ID / campus UID was a required field.
2. **Attach the itemized paid receipt.** Describe the expense as the event,
   not the bar/restaurant bill (e.g. "Freehouse event", not "Freehouse bar tab").
3. **Receipts should show the payer's name and the card's last four digits.**
4. **Attendance field = actual turnout**, not RSVP count or room capacity.
5. **Payout = direct deposit via the ASUC/GA EFT authorization** (a DocuSign
   form on CalLink), with bank proof: a monthly checking statement or voided
   check was required. Bank details go through the secure form, never chat.
   The first payment after enrollment may still arrive as a check (ASUC AP/AR,
   432A Eshleman Hall, Cal ID needed - verify current hours).
6. **After submission, a different club Financial Agent must advance the
   request** before approval and payment. Track it to payment; a nudge email
   to the finance officer (drafted for approval first) is acceptable.

## Proof-of-payment extras

If ASUC asks for separate proof (e.g. a Venmo-paid vendor charge): an unaltered
bank statement or transaction record with the charge highlighted and last four
digits visible - a current, minimal record, never a statement exposing full
account numbers. Whether Venmo-paid vendor purchases are acceptable is a
current-policy question: verify before promising.

## Hard rules

- No bank details, card numbers, or login credentials in chat, ever.
- File under the club whose funds pay out; the payee is whoever actually paid.
- When a required document or field cannot be produced (missing itemized
  receipt, unverified enrollment), stop and tell the organizer what is missing
  rather than filing a partial request and hoping.
