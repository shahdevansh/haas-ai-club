# Marketing message (WhatsApp / Slack)

Current style (organizer instruction, Nov 2026): short and crisp, title, venue
and time at the top, using emojis. This supersedes an older plain-label format.

## Format

    <Event title> <hook emoji>

    🗓 <time, weekday, date + timezone if the audience spans zones>
    📍 <room, building - only once confirmed> / 💻 <online>
    🎤 <speaker(s), company>

    <2-3 short lines on why it matters, aimed at MBA roles - PMs, marketers, GTM>

    <join line, e.g. "Please join Haas AI Club for a lunch & learn with <speaker> on <what>!">

    RSVP link: <public CampusGroups RSVP URL>

## Rules

- Short. Every line earns its place. Emojis for structure, not decoration.
- The venue line appears only once confirmed; for online events, say online -
  the link itself goes on the RSVP, not in the blast.
- Free events can say so; paid/restricted events state the actual terms.
- The link is the public RSVP link - open it and check it resolves to this
  event before handing the message over.
- The organizer usually sends these themselves. Deliver the text; do not post
  to group chats on their behalf without an explicit ask and approval of the
  exact words and destination.
